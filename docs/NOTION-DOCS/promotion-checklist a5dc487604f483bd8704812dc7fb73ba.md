# Promotion Checklist

## Draft to Research

- Artifact has owner, type, ID, and summary.
- RDD inputs are linked.
- Initial sources, hypotheses, or experiment plans are listed.
- Open questions are captured in bottom matter.

## Research to Validated

- Research findings are reflected in spec or validation criteria.
- Manifest validates against the living-document schema.
- At least one validation plan exists.
- RDD requirements map to acceptance criteria.

## Validated to Canonical

- Validation evidence is attached or linked.
- Geometry, semantic, CRS, and regression checks have explicit outcomes.
- Reviewer approval is recorded.
- Supersession behavior is defined.
